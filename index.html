ⓘ Important — Please Read Before Using

HealthyChex  ·  Preventative Health Checklist Tool

⚠ Not a Medical Device

HealthyChex is an informational wellness tool only. It is not a medical device, not a diagnostic tool, and not a substitute for the advice, diagnosis, or treatment of a licensed physician or other qualified healthcare provider. Always consult your doctor before making any healthcare decisions.

🔒 Your Data & Privacy

HealthyChex stores your information locally on this device only using your browser's built-in storage (localStorage). Your data is:

  • Never transmitted to any server or third party
  • Never shared with advertisers, insurers, or employers
  • Only accessible on this browser on this device
  • Deletable at any time using the "Clear all data" button

⚠ HIPAA Notice

HealthyChex is not a HIPAA-covered entity and does not provide HIPAA-compliant data storage. The health information you enter is considered personal use consumer health data governed by your browser's local storage, not protected health information (PHI) under HIPAA. Do not use this tool to store or share information in a clinical or provider setting without appropriate safeguards.

📋 Clinical Guidelines

Recommendations are based on USPSTF and CDC ACIP 2025 guidelines for average-risk adults. They may not apply to your individual health circumstances. Your physician may recommend different screening intervals based on your personal and family medical history.

👤 Who Should Use This Tool

HealthyChex is intended for adults aged 18 and over seeking a personalised summary of recommended preventative health screenings. It is not intended for use by or on behalf of minors, and is not a clinical decision support tool for healthcare providers.

🕐 No Liability

The creators of HealthyChex make no warranties, express or implied, regarding the accuracy, completeness, or fitness for purpose of this tool. Use is entirely at your own risk. The tool is provided "as is" for personal informational purposes only.
